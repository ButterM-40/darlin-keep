Repair an Item

Records the repair and spends the smith's tokens and ingots.

Repair Details

Repair checks restore one damage unit at the first success threshold, plus another for every additional interval. Both weapons and armor cost one crafting token per attempt; weapon repairs use one ingot, while armor repairs scale with defense value.

The PDF does not specify whether a failed crafting/repair check consumes materials. This app defaults to consuming resources on every recorded attempt; you can change this in Settings.
